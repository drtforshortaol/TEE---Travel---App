Master Itinerary Protocols

Night Before Each Stop

  • Open Daily Operations and confirm the next-day plan.
  • Open Transportation for departure time, buffer, ticket/pass, and baggage notes.
  • Open Hotels for check-in, address, luggage storage, and problem plan.
  • Open Private Documents only when exact confirmation numbers or private references are needed.

Travel Day Flags

  • Use the Move / Transition Days filter first.
  • Confirm transfer sequence: hotel → station/airport → next city → hotel.
  • Keep language, money/tipping, weather, and packing available for the destination country.

What Matters Today?

  • Current city and sleeping location.
  • Next movement and timing buffer.
  • Tickets/reservations that must be ready.
  • Weather/clothing and end-of-day repack needs.
