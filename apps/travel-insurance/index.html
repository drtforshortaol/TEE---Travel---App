Insurance Protocols

Emergency Medical Steps

  • Get immediate medical or safety help first; do not delay urgent care.
  • Contact the insurer assistance line as soon as practical after the situation is stable.
  • Save medical reports, invoices, medication receipts, doctor names, and facility contact information.
  • Use Private Documents for exact policy and assistance details.

Travel Delay / Cancellation Steps

  • Ask airline, rail, hotel, or tour provider for written proof of delay/cancellation when possible.
  • Save receipts for meals, hotels, taxis, replacement transport, and required purchases.
  • Record date, time, carrier, route, and reason given for the disruption.
  • Check Transportation and Daily Operations before committing to backup plans.

Lost Baggage Steps

  • File a baggage report with the carrier before leaving the airport/station if possible.
  • Photograph baggage tags, report forms, damaged items, and replacement-purchase receipts.
  • Keep purchases reasonable and clearly tied to the delay/loss.
  • Check Packing for essential replacement items.

Medical / Pharmacy Notes

  • Carry medications in the day bag or carry-on, not only in checked luggage.
  • Use Local Knowledge and Language for pharmacy, doctor, and help phrases.
  • Keep medication names, allergies, and important health notes private but accessible.

Policy / Coverage Checklist

  • Confirm covered travelers, coverage dates, destination countries, and plan name before departure.
  • Confirm emergency medical, evacuation, trip interruption, delay, and baggage coverage.
  • Store the full policy, assistance card, and claim rules in Private Documents and the phone PDF.

Claim Documentation Checklist

  • Save receipts, invoices, reports, emails, chat transcripts, and case numbers.
  • Photograph damaged items, baggage tags, tickets, boarding passes, and provider notices.
  • Write a simple timeline while events are fresh: what happened, when, where, and who helped.

Who to Contact First

  • For medical emergencies: local emergency services / medical provider first, insurer assistance second.
  • For delays/cancellations: carrier or hotel first, then insurer if costs or interruption are involved.
  • For baggage: carrier baggage desk first, insurer later with the carrier report.
  • For exact numbers and references, open Private Documents.

What to Save

  • Receipts, photos, medical reports, police reports, airline/rail reports, hotel receipts, and taxi receipts.
  • Names of staff, dates/times, location, case/reference numbers, and contact emails.
  • Use Daily Operations notes to log the situation before details are forgotten.
