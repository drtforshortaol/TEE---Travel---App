Enchanting Travels Suggestions — Universal Packing List

Reference suggestions from Enchanting Travels, kept separate from Glenn’s master packing list and ChatGPT-developed TEE packing guidance.

Important: This is not the TEE master packing list. It is a source-reference checklist from Enchanting Travels for comparison and omission checking only.

Core suggestions from the source

Documents

  • Passport / visas
  • Travel insurance documents
  • Tickets / itinerary
  • Emergency contacts
  • Cards and cash

Electronics

  • Phone
  • Chargers
  • Power bank
  • Travel adapters
  • Other trip-specific gadgets

Health / medical

  • Prescription and essential medications
  • Basic first-aid items
  • Health items appropriate to destination and activities

Clothing

  • Weather-appropriate clothing
  • Rain layer
  • Warm layer / fleece where needed
  • Comfortable walking clothing

Shoes

  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Activity-appropriate shoes
  • Sandals / alternate footwear when useful

Toiletries

  • Personal hygiene basics
  • Sun protection
  • Trip-specific toiletry needs

Miscellaneous

  • Water bottle
  • Sunglasses
  • Daypack / small carry bag
  • Useful destination-specific accessories

Carry-on

  • Essential medications
  • Identity / travel documents
  • Critical electronics
  • Backup clothing / essentials in case checked luggage is delayed

Special-trip categories included by Enchanting Travels

The Universal Packing List also contains suggestions for trekking, tropical travel, temperate/polar travel, beach/cruise travel, safari, religious visits and other trip types. Use only the sections that apply to this itinerary.
