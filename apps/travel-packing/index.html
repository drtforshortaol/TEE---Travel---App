Packing Protocols

Before Departure Checklist

Confirm passport access, phone chargers, medication pouch, weather layers, rain gear, shoes, and private document access before leaving home.

Day Bag Checklist

Carry phone, charger/battery, water, rain layer, sunglasses, light warmth layer, medication basics, tissue, hand sanitizer, and the day’s transportation/hotel notes.

Travel-Day Repack

Night before every move: separate tomorrow’s clothes, charging kit, documents, meds, snacks, rain layer, and luggage items needed before check-in.

Weather Layer System

Use light base layers, fleece/warm layer, waterproof shell, hat/gloves for alpine or sunrise days, and grippy shoes for wet/stone/boardwalk surfaces.

Laundry / Rewear Plan

Pack repeatable outfits and quick-dry basics. Recheck laundry windows after Croatia/Slovenia and before the Switzerland segment.

Electronics / Chargers / Adapters

Keep a lean kit: phone charger, watch charger, USB-A/USB-C cables, battery, adapter/converter, earbuds, and backup charging plan for plane/train days.

Medication / Health Items

Carry essential meds in the personal bag, plus cough/allergy/stomach basics, eye drops, lip balm, Band-Aids, and backup supply in suitcase.

Documents / Private Documents Reminder

Keep sensitive references outside public versions. Use Private Documents for confirmation numbers, travel IDs, insurance references, and confidential quick-access notes.

Luggage Loss Readiness

Before departure photograph each checked bag inside and out, record brand/color/distinguishing marks, place contact information inside the bag, test any tracker, and keep essentials/medications/IDs/electronics in carry-on. At check-in photograph the airline bag tag and keep claim-tag receipts until the bag is safely recovered.

Final Hotel Room Sweep

Before every checkout: outlet chargers, bathroom, nightstand, safe, closet, under bed, documents, medications, phone/watch, and rain/warm layer.
