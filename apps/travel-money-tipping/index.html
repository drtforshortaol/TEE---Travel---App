Money + Tipping Protocols

Repeatable travel rules

Country Currency Quick Guide

  • Turkey: Turkish lira / TRY / ₺.
  • Croatia, Slovenia, Austria: euro / EUR / €.
  • Switzerland: Swiss franc / CHF.
  • Keep currencies separated in the wallet to avoid using the wrong cash quickly.

Card vs Cash Strategy

  • Use credit card for hotels, larger restaurants, transportation counters, shops, and bigger purchases.
  • Use small cash for bathrooms, tips, small cafes, markets, taxis, kiosks, and emergency backup.
  • When a terminal asks, choose the local currency instead of USD conversion.

ATM / Cash Withdrawal Notes

  • Prefer bank ATMs or ATMs attached to banks.
  • Avoid unknown standalone tourist ATMs when possible.
  • Decline dynamic currency conversion if offered.
  • Withdraw moderate amounts so you are not carrying excessive cash.

Small Bills / Coins Reminder

  • Keep a small coin/bill pocket for bathrooms, cafes, luggage help, and small tips.
  • Before leaving each hotel, check whether you have enough small local cash for the day.
  • Switzerland and euro countries may require coins for some bathrooms or lockers.

Restaurant / Hotel / Guide Tipping

  • Restaurants: use the country card below for local norms; avoid automatic US-style over-tipping.
  • Hotels: keep small cash for housekeeping and luggage help when useful.
  • Guides/drivers: tip more when service is personal, long, or especially helpful.
  • In Austria and Switzerland, rounding up is often more natural than leaving a large table tip.

Daily Cash Carry Plan

  • Morning check: card, backup card, small local cash, and coins.
  • Move days: carry enough cash for taxis, bathrooms, luggage help, snacks, and unexpected delays.
  • Keep exact payment references, confirmation numbers, and sensitive account details in Private Documents.
Private note: this app gives practical cash/card and tipping guidance. It does not store card numbers, bank logins, or full payment credentials.
