Source Document Manager

This is the document control center. Use it to see what original documents TEE has retained, whether they still need attention, what structured records were created from them, where those records belong, and how to get back to the original when needed.

Standard workflow: 1 Add source → 2 Structure → 3 Review → 4 Approve/Commit → 5 Verify destination → 6 Archive when complete.

The simple rule: Source Document Manager manages the original and its processing history. Structured Documents holds the organized extraction. Flights, Hotels, Rail, Insurance, Identity, and other TEE apps are where you use the resulting information during the trip.

This is the known source-document inventory. Protected originals are intentionally not bundled into the public-safe app package.
