Enchanting Travels Suggestions — General Travel Checklist

General preparation suggestions from Enchanting Travels, kept separate from TEE’s Adaptive Checklist and Glenn/ChatGPT-developed procedures.

Important: This is a reference checklist from Enchanting Travels. It does not replace the cumulative Adaptive Checklist or TEE’s existing travel-day protocols.

Before the trip

  • Confirm traveler names and dates of birth match passports.
  • Review and report allergies, dietary needs and mobility restrictions.
  • Confirm self-booked flights and airline PNRs are recorded.
  • Review itinerary details, hotels, room types, meals and included activities.
  • Review travel-insurance arrangements.
  • Check visa / entry-document requirements and document validity.
  • Download or save final travel documents for offline access.
  • Make any desired restaurant reservations.
  • Select airline seats when available.
  • Print or save any documents you may want available without internet.
  • Keep emergency contact information available.

Flight-day / disruption reminders

  • Check in with the airline about 24 hours before departure when available.
  • If a flight is delayed or canceled, contact or work with the airline first.
  • Then update the Trip Coordinator / travel support contact as needed.
