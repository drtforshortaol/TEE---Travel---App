Daily Operations ProtocolsGeneral travel-day rhythm and how to use this app.Expand

Morning

  • Use the open Today card as the primary traveler screen.
  • Work down the numbered sections in order.
  • Add unexpected tasks to Adaptive Checklist.

Night before

  • Use the open Tomorrow card to stage luggage, clothing, documents, money, and timing.
  • Charge devices and prepare the day bag.
  • If Vault authorization is active, tomorrow’s relevant protected booking details are shown directly in the card.

Protected details

When the Vault is locked, Shared/Private values are omitted. After authorization, relevant secure details appear directly inside Today and Tomorrow for the remainder of the authorized session. Reveal/Collapse remains available.
