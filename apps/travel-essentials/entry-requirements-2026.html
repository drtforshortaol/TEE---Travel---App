Final Entry Requirements — Sep/Oct 2026

Traveler-facing checklist for U.S. ordinary-passport holders on this TEE trip. Checked against current official U.S., Turkish and EU guidance on Sep. 7, 2026.

1. Passport check — do this before departure

Each traveler must carry the physical passport.

Schengen legal minimum
Passport valid at least 3 months after leaving Schengen on Oct. 6 → at least Jan. 6, 2027.
TEE conservative safety rule
Because U.S. guidance recommends 6 months and current Switzerland guidance is stricter, use Apr. 2, 2027 or later as the practical pass/fail target.
  • Passport was issued within the previous 10 years when entering Schengen.
  • Have blank space available for border stamps.
  • Passport name matches airline tickets and traveler records.

2. Türkiye

No tourist visa required for U.S. ordinary-passport holders for stays up to 90 days in any 180-day period.

Carry the passport and keep enough blank stamp space. Your trip length is well within the visa-exempt period.

3. Croatia · Slovenia · Austria · Switzerland

No tourist visa required for U.S. citizens for a short Schengen stay of up to 90 days in any 180-day period.

Border officials may ask for proof of lodging, onward/return travel and sufficient funds, so TEE/Vault access to hotel and transportation records is useful.

4. EES — active, but no application to complete

The EU Entry/Exit System is fully operational. At the external Schengen border, non-EU short-stay travelers may have passport data, facial image and fingerprints registered.

No advance EES application is required. Allow a little extra time at the first Schengen external-border entry.

5. ETIAS — not required for this trip

ETIAS is not in operation as of Sep. 7, 2026 and the EU says it is scheduled for the last quarter of 2026. No ETIAS application is being accepted now.

Do not pay any third-party ETIAS site.

6. Final traveler packet

  • Physical passport for each traveler.
  • TEE/Vault access to hotel confirmations and onward/return transportation.
  • Offline copy of key itinerary/booking information.
  • Travel insurance and emergency-contact access.

This page intentionally contains no passport numbers, DOBs, booking references or other protected values.
