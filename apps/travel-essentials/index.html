Lost Phone / Phone StolenFind it, protect it, then preserve service and accounts.

Set up before departure

  1. On each iPhone: Settings → [name] → Find My → Find My iPhone.
  2. Turn on Find My iPhone, Find My network, and Send Last Location.
  3. Consider enabling Stolen Device Protection.
  4. Make sure a companion knows how to reach iCloud.com/find. Apple says Find Devices can be used there without the normal verification-code step when the trusted phone itself is missing.
  5. Keep carrier and recovery information in the encrypted Vault. Record serial/IMEI and Apple Account recovery details privately if desired.

If the phone disappears

  1. Use another device to open Find My or iCloud.com/find; locate it and play a sound if appropriate.
  2. If not recovered quickly, activate Lost Mode.
  3. Call carrier global support at 1-800-711-8300 if the line needs assistance or suspension.
  4. If stolen, report it to local police. Keep the police reference for insurance or replacement claims.
  5. Do not remove the phone from Find My / your Apple Account; doing so can remove Activation Lock.
  6. Do not give anyone your passcode, Apple Account password, or verification code because they claim to have found the phone.

Lost Wallet / Credit CardKnow exactly what was carried and how to shut it down.

Set up before departure

  • Complete the private Wallet Inventory in the encrypted Vault before departure. Status: pending.
  • For every card carried, create a private Credit Card record with issuer, last four digits, lost/stolen customer-service number, and overseas/international assistance number.
  • Keep at least one backup payment card physically separate from the primary wallet.
  • Record which IDs, insurance cards, cash and other important items are normally in the wallet.

If lost or stolen

  1. Open Wallet Inventory and identify exactly what is missing.
  2. Lock/freeze affected cards in issuer apps when available, then call the issuer.
  3. Use the separately stored backup card/cash.
  4. If theft is suspected, obtain a police report/reference.
  5. Replace identity/insurance items as needed; use Passport and Insurance sections for those specific problems.
Pre-travel item still required: issuer-specific card contacts cannot be considered complete until the actual travel cards are entered in the Vault.

Luggage — Prepare Before TravelMake a missing bag identifiable and a claim easy to prove.

Before leaving home

  • Complete a private Luggage Profile for each checked bag before departure: owner, brand, color, size, distinctive marks and tracker information if used. Status: pending.
  • Photograph the exterior and interior/contents before departure.
  • Place traveler contact information inside the bag as well as on the outside tag.
  • Keep passports, medications, valuables, important electronics and one change of essentials in carry-on/personal items.
  • Reconfirm baggage allowance shortly before departure. Current trip source notes indicate two checked pieces each on the principal international booking, but the airline app remains the source of truth.

At airport check-in

  • Photograph the bag after the airline tag is attached.
  • Keep baggage-claim receipts/tags until every checked bag is safely recovered.
  • Add the airline bag tag number to the Luggage Profile if desired.

If delayed or lost

  1. Report the bag to the airline baggage desk before leaving the airport.
  2. Obtain the written Property Irregularity Report / claim reference.
  3. Keep baggage tags, boarding pass, screenshots and every receipt for reasonable replacement essentials.
  4. Use Insurance for any applicable delayed/lost-baggage claim.
