Essentials Protocols

Departure Readiness

  • Confirm passports, tickets, insurance, cards, phone/data, medications, and chargers.
  • Confirm private documents are available offline before leaving home.
  • Confirm first hotel and first transportation move are easy to find.

Traveler Cards Reminder

  • Create traveler cards for Couple A traveler, Vicky, Couple B traveler, and Couple B traveler when ready.
  • Keep sensitive ID details in the Secure Vault Identity section.
  • Use cards for practical notes, emergency contacts, and travel preferences.

Passport / ID Reminder

  • Physical passport is the primary document; digital copies are backup only.
  • Check passport location before every hotel departure and transportation day.
  • Use the Secure Vault Identity section for encrypted Passport and Global Entry records.

Phone / Data Reminder

  • Confirm international plan, roaming settings, charger, battery bank, and offline maps.
  • Keep carrier support and phone/data details in Secure Vault.
  • Test key PDFs and app pages offline before travel.

Insurance / Medications / Health

  • Confirm travel insurance access, emergency contact, and claim process location.
  • Pack medications in carry-on/day bag, not checked luggage only.
  • Keep medical notes minimal unless needed, and private.

Money / Cards / Cash

  • Carry a small cash reserve and at least one backup card strategy.
  • Use Money + Tipping for country guidance and Costs for budget tracking.
  • Keep sensitive account details out of daily cards; use Secure Vault if needed.

Travel Day Critical Checklist

  • Passport, wallet, phone, charger, tickets, hotel address, medication, glasses, and day bag.
  • Confirm next movement, departure time, arrival location, and backup option.
  • Open Daily Operations before leaving the hotel.
