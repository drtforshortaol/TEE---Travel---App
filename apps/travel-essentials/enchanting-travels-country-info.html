Turkey — Enchanting Travels suggestions

Money & cards

  • Currency: Turkish lira (TRY).
  • Visa and Mastercard are widely accepted; American Express is accepted by some businesses.
  • ATMs are common in larger towns and cities; carry cash for more remote areas.

Tipping

  • Guide: TRY 600 per guest/day.
  • Chauffeur: TRY 300 per guest/day.
  • Tour manager: TRY 1,000 per guest/day.
  • Hotel porter: TRY 200 per guest/day.
  • Restaurant: 10% if service charge is not included.

Typical costs listed

  • Water: TRY 11.
  • Coffee: TRY 90.
  • Beer: TRY 120–150.
  • Dinner for two: TRY 1,500–2,000.

Phone, power & safety

  • Country code: +90.
  • Wi‑Fi generally available; local SIM guidance is included in the source.
  • Electrical: Type C/F, 230 V, 50 Hz.
  • Emergency number: 112.
  • Source recommends bottled water while traveling.
  • Be alert for shopping pressure, pickpocketing and card fraud.

Croatia — Enchanting Travels suggestions

Money & cards

  • The source notes that Croatia adopted the euro on January 1, 2023.
  • Visa and Mastercard are commonly accepted; American Express is described as rarely accepted.
  • ATMs are widely available; carry some cash in remote areas.

Tipping

  • Guide: EUR 10–15/group half-day; EUR 15–20/group full-day.
  • Chauffeur: EUR 2–4/person/day.
  • Tour manager: EUR 10–15/group half-day; EUR 15–20/group full-day.
  • Porter: EUR 1–2/bag.
  • Restaurant: 10–20%.

Typical costs listed

  • Water: EUR 1.30.
  • Coffee: EUR 2.
  • Beer: EUR 5.
  • Dinner for two: EUR 35–45.

Phone, power & practical notes

  • Country code: +385.
  • Wi‑Fi widely available; SIM information is included in the source.
  • Electrical: Type C/F, 230 V, 50 Hz.
  • Source includes national-park, bus and advance-booking reminders.
  • Source cautions against forced shopping situations.
