Summary

Eurail Pass Rules & What To Do — practical trip-use summary for your mobile Global Flex Pass.

At the station / on the train

  • Use the live ticket in the Eurail Rail Planner app. A screenshot of the dynamic ticket/QR is not valid for travel.
  • Keep the physical passport or ID linked to the pass available for inspection.
  • The confirmation email by itself is not a valid travel document.

Before boarding on a travel day

  • Save the planned journey to My Trip in the Rail Planner app.
  • Activate the correct travel day before using the pass for that day.
  • Open My Pass → Show ticket when inspected. The app generates the day ticket/QR for that travel day.
  • A Flex Pass uses one travel day for covered train travel during that calendar day, so do not activate an extra day unnecessarily.

Seat reservations and separate tickets

  • The Eurail pass and a seat reservation are separate things. Some trains require or recommend a reservation even though the rail fare is covered by the pass.
  • Follow the reservation status shown for the specific train: required, recommended, or optional.
  • If a reservation was issued separately, keep that reservation/ticket accessible in the required format.
  • Some reservations are e-tickets; others may be paper tickets sent by mail. Follow the instructions on the reservation itself about printing.
  • If an expected e-ticket/reservation does not appear, allow time for it to populate in the Eurail account and contact Eurail Customer Service if it remains missing.

If plans change

  • If you take a different train, update the journey in Rail Planner so the journey attached to the pass matches what you actually ride.
  • Do not cancel a travel day unless you are certain you will not use it and the app still allows cancellation.
  • If you miss a connection, use Rail Planner/station information to identify the next valid train, then make sure the replacement journey is saved to My Trip.
  • If the replacement train requires a reservation, obtain that reservation before boarding when required.

TEE trip reminder

  • For your active Part 2 rail days, open Rail Planner before leaving the hotel and verify that the correct day and journeys are active.
  • Keep any separately issued paper/A4 rail documents with the travel packet.
  • TEE may show train times for quick reference, but Rail Planner is the travel-ticket source.
