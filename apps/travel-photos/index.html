Nightly Photo Routine

Use this at the end of each travel day. Target time: 3–5 minutes. The goal is memory capture, not final photo sorting.

  1. Open today’s photos. On the iPhone, open Photos and quickly scroll through today’s images.
  2. Favorite the best 5–15 photos. Prioritize best view, group/person photo, food photo, hotel or room photo, train/airport/station clue, receipt/document photo, and anything funny, beautiful, or memorable.
  3. Open the Travel Hub Photos app. Go to Travel Hub → Photos → Daily Photo Notes.
  4. Enter a consistent day label. Examples: Day 01 — Travel Day, Day 02 — Istanbul, Day 04 — Cappadocia.
  5. Tap Load / New Template. This loads the daily photo note template or reopens the saved entry for that day.
  6. Fill only what matters. Use short phrases for best photos, food photos, hotel/room photos, transportation photos, receipts/documents, people/group photos, caption ideas, and problems/things to remember.
  7. Tap Save Entry. Confirm the day appears under Saved daily entries.
  8. Stop there. Do not overwork it. Favorite photos first, write a short note second, save entry, go to sleep.

Photo Reference Guides

Use these as quick reminders. They are grouped here so the Photos app stays clean.

Photo Capture Plan

  • Capture now; sort later.
  • Take orientation photos first: hotel exterior, street sign, station/airport sign, and one wide view.
  • Take memory photos second: people, meals, scenery, landmarks, and small details that explain the day.

Daily Photo Checklist

  • Morning: battery, storage, and lens wipe.
  • During the day: one context photo, one people photo, one food/detail photo, and one favorite view.
  • Evening: favorite the best photos and save a short daily note.

Hotel / Room

  • Photo hotel exterior, room number if helpful, room layout, and room problems on arrival.
  • Photo baggage location before leaving bags with a hotel.

Transportation / Station

  • Photo departure boards, platform signs, baggage storage, and station/airport landmarks.
  • Photo taxi/rideshare pickup location when useful.

Food / Restaurant

  • Photo restaurant exterior/name, menu highlights, favorite dishes, and receipt if useful.
  • Note restaurants to remember, avoid, or recommend.

Landmark / Memory

  • Pair wide establishing photos with details: signs, textures, food, transit, weather, and people.
  • Take one “end of city” favorite photo before moving to the next destination.

Receipt / Document

  • Photo receipts, tickets, luggage tags, damage reports, insurance paperwork, and lost item forms.
  • Do not place sensitive photos into a public repo.

Find Later Notes

  • When a photo matters, take a second photo of a sign, receipt, menu, or map so it is searchable later.
  • Use the daily note box for caption ideas and missing shots.

Trip Archive Link

  • After the vacation is complete, move the best images and saved daily notes into the Trip Archive workflow.
  • Prioritize favorites by city, hotel, restaurant, transportation lesson, and memorable experience.
